Plumber in Wallace Woods, Covington, KY

Wallace Woods grew from a farm into a neighborhood of mixed homes, large gardens and tree-lined streets. Outdoor water, long supply runs and big houses shape the plumbing calls here.

Garden neighborhoodMixed home stylesTurnaround Park

From farm to neighborhood

The Covington Neighborhood Collaborative says Wallace Woods was developed around 1895 from the Wallace family farm at what was then the edge of Covington. It describes mixed house styles and sizes, including Colonial Revival, Arts and Crafts, Italianate and Victorian, with many homes showing Romanesque elements.

A few larger homes were built for wealthy Cincinnati mercantile families, and smaller homes were built for the workers who constructed them. Many homes have elaborate gardens, and the association has been active for over 30 years. Turnaround Park is at 2234 Oakland Avenue.

Outdoor spigots

Garden hose bibs are where winter damage often starts. Disconnect hoses before cold weather and shut off the inside valve if you have one.

Buried lines and hidden leaks

A leak on a buried supply line may never show on the surface. A meter that moves with every fixture off is the first clue.

Long runs in larger homes

Distance between the heater and the farthest tap can mean long waits for hot water. That is a design question before it is a fault.

Alley and yard drainage

The association has worked on alley drainage. That is a stormwater matter, so call the city or the district for public drains and a plumber for pipes on your side.

Water and sewer basics

Northern Kentucky Water District serves 99 percent of Kenton County households. Ask the district where the utility side of your service ends and yours begins, because the owner is responsible for the water line from the meter to the house. Our water line repair page explains how those jobs are scoped.

The Kentucky Division of Plumbing says water service from the meter to the house needs a permit. Permit rules vary, so ask the division or the local plumbing inspector before any work starts.
