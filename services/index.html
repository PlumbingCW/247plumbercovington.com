Find the service that matches the problem

Plumbing calls usually begin with a symptom: a gurgle, a puddle, a cold shower or a water bill that makes no sense. The pages below start from those symptoms and explain how the cause is found before the repair is chosen. Pick the one closest to your problem and read how it works, or call and describe it.

Drain trouble starts with drain cleaning for single fixtures and moves to sewer line cleaning and camera inspection when the main line is involved. Water heater trouble runs from repair through replacement to tankless units. Hidden water is covered by leak detection, water line repair and burst pipe repair.

Emergency work and planned work

Some services are marked with an emergency page because the damage grows by the hour. Those pages open with what to do while you wait. Others, such as repiping, plumbing installation and basement waterproofing, are planned jobs where the useful thing is a clear explanation and a clear price.

Whichever you need, the line is the same: call, describe the problem and hear the price before work starts. Businesses should read about commercial plumbing, and homeowners with a failing pump can go straight to sump pump repair.
