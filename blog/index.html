What to do first: read, then decide if you need a plumber

These guides explain how a plumbing job works, what you can safely check yourself and when a licensed plumber is the better call. Each article ends with a note on what applies in Covington, KY, drawn from local sources such as Sanitation District No. 1 and the Kentucky Division of Plumbing.

Topics move from simple checks, like the EPA's two-hour meter test, to jobs that need a permit or an inspection. If a guide describes your problem and you are unsure, stop and phone the 24/7 line. A short call is cheaper than a mistake behind a wall. When something is already flooding, skip reading and go to the services overview or call right away.
