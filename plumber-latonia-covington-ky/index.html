Plumber in Latonia, Covington, KY

Latonia has the largest business district outside downtown Covington, with homes on the streets around it. A plumbing call here can come from a bungalow, a shop or a ball field.

Ritte's Corner districtHomes and businessesZIP code 41015

A neighborhood with its own downtown

The Covington Neighborhood Collaborative says Latonia has the largest business district in Covington outside downtown, centered on Ritte's Corner, which is a National Register historic district. Two shopping centers sit farther south on former racetrack land near I-275.

The Latonia Racetrack ran from 1883 to 1939. Housing on the same page includes cottages and larger homes from the turn of the 20th century, Craftsman bungalows from the 1920s and 1930s and a handful of large Victorians called Painted Ladies.

Homes, shops and parks in one service area

Latonia calls tend to fall into a few groups.

House drains

Kitchen and bathroom drains in bungalows and cottages usually start slowly. A cable run often clears them before they stop completely.

Shop and restaurant lines

Businesses along the main strip have restrooms, kitchens and sometimes grease. Those need different tools than a house sink.

Outdoor spigots near parks

Bill Cappel Sports Complex, Rosendale Park and 35th and Carlisle Park are in the neighborhood. If your yard hose bib leaks, shut it off inside before cold weather.

Water heater age

Tanks have a service life, so ask your plumber how old yours is. Age does not mean failure, but it changes the repair versus replace decision.

Latonia, 41015 and Taylor Mill

Latonia is part of Covington, but the 41015 ZIP code is also used for Taylor Mill addresses, which is a separate city. If a map or a mailing label confuses the two, give the street name and cross street when you call. Our Taylor Mill page covers that city.

Sanitation District No. 1 says older parts of Covington have combined sewers. Ask the district at 859-578-7450 whether your street is on one, and if several drains back up together, read about sewer line repair.
